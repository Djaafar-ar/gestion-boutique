# Credits

Gestion Boutique started from **flutter_billing_app** by
**Dinesh Sowndar** (Dinesh-Sowndar):
https://github.com/Dinesh-Sowndar/flutter_billing_app

Thank you, Dinesh, for sharing your billing app and for allowing us to
use your code freely. Your barcode scanning, cart and Bluetooth receipt
printing were the starting point of this shop manager.

Since then the app has been largely rewritten (database, stock, users,
suppliers, customer credit, returns, alerts, statistics, Wi-Fi sync,
Google Sheet, backups, Bluetooth scanner...). The few remaining parts of
the original code will also be rewritten (see `docs/license_check.md`
and Phase 9 in `PLAN.md`), so that all the code belongs to the shop
owner, who plans to sell the app.

Other open-source packages used by the app are listed in `pubspec.yaml`;
each keeps its own license.
